What this is

A shared budget tracker for a household or group: tracking budgets/wallets per person, transactions, and receipts. Each household's data is isolated from every other household.

Accounts

A household account is shared by everyone in that household using one username and password. You're responsible for keeping those credentials safe and for what happens under your account.

Children's data

Children never create their own login or provide their own data. Any information about a child (name, spending, etc.) is entered and controlled entirely by the adult who owns the household account.

Your data, your control

You can export your data at any time (CSV, Excel, or PDF) and permanently delete your household account and all its data at any time from within the app.
