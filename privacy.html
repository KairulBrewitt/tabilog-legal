Privacy Policy

What we collect

On the native (mobile) app, all data stays on your device: people, budgets, transactions, and receipt photos are stored in a local database and never transmitted anywhere. The only exception is if you choose to submit feedback, which is sent to the app's operator.

On the hosted web version, we collect: household name, username, and password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash, never in plain text). Names of people you add, and any transactions, categories, descriptions, and receipt photos you enter for them.

How it's used

Solely to provide the budgeting service to your household. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

How it's stored

On the native app, in an on-device database only. On the hosted web version, in a hosted database, transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Optional feedback you submit is stored separately and is only ever visible to the app's operator, not to other households.

Children's data

We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. Any data about a child is entered by the adult household owner, who controls and can delete it at any time.

Your rights

Export your data at any time (CSV, Excel, or PDF), or permanently delete your household and all associated data at any time from within the app.
